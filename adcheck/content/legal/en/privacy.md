# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {date}_

This policy explains what {app} ("we") collects and why.

## What we collect
- **Account data:** e-mail address, sign-in method (e-mail or Google), language preference.
- **Content you submit:** ad images, ad texts, product descriptions, landing page URLs, rejection reasons, and the reports, images and letters we generate.
- **Payment data:** plan, amount, coin, status, transaction hash and order number. We never see your wallet's private keys or your exchange credentials.
- **Technical data:** basic logs (IP address, browser, errors) used for security and rate limiting.

## Why we use it
To provide the Service, keep your history, manage your plan and credits, send transactional e-mails (payment confirmations, renewal reminders), prevent abuse, and comply with the law. We do not sell your data and we do not use your ads to advertise.

## Who processes it
- **Supabase** — database, authentication and file storage.
- **Our AI model provider** — analysis of ads and writing of texts.
- **OpenAI or Replicate** — image generation.
- **NOWPayments** — crypto payments.
- **Resend** — e-mail delivery.
- **Vercel** — hosting.
These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and security measures. Data may be processed outside your country.

## Landing pages
If you give a landing page URL, our server downloads the public page once to compare it with your ad.

## Retention
We keep your content while your account exists so you can see your history. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time; payment records may be kept as required for accounting.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to some processing. Write to {email}.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit, images are stored privately and shown through short-lived links, and access to each user's data is restricted at the database level.

## Children
The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.
