# Refund Policy

_Last updated: {date}_

Payments to {app} are made in cryptocurrency through NOWPayments. Because blockchain transactions cannot be reversed by us, refunds work as follows.

## Activated plans
Once a plan is activated, the payment is **not refundable**, including for unused credits or time, except in the cases below.

## When we refund
- **Duplicate payment:** you paid twice for the same order.
- **Overpayment:** you sent clearly more than the amount due.
- **Service not delivered:** a technical problem on our side prevented you from using your paid plan and we could not fix it within 7 days.

## Partial payments
If you sent less than the amount due, your plan is not activated. You can send the remaining amount from the payment page, or contact us within 14 days to either receive a refund of what was received or apply it to a cheaper plan.

## Expired or failed payments
If a payment arrives after the invoice expired, contact us with your order number; we will activate the plan or refund it.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are sent in the same coin and network you used, to an address you give us, minus the network fee. They are calculated on the USD value of the order. We process valid requests within 10 business days.

## Wrong network or address
Funds sent on a network other than the one shown on the payment page, or to a wrong address, may be lost and cannot always be recovered.

## How to ask
E-mail {email} with your order number (shown on your Subscription page) and your wallet address for the refund.
