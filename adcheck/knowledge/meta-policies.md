# Meta Advertising Standards — Working Summary for Ad Review

> This is an internal, condensed summary of Meta's Advertising Standards (Facebook, Instagram,
> Messenger, Audience Network) written to guide automated pre-publication review. It is not the
> official text. Meta updates its policies often; when in doubt, the reviewer should flag the risk
> and recommend that the advertiser check the current official policy page
> (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/).

Meta reviews the **whole ad**: image/video, headline, primary text, call-to-action, targeting, the
product or service itself, and the landing page. An ad can be rejected for any one of these parts.

---

## 1. How Meta evaluates ads (key principles)

1. **The product decides the category.** A perfectly worded ad is still rejected if the product
   itself is prohibited (e.g. weapons, drugs). Restricted products need extra conditions
   (authorization, age targeting, country rules).
2. **Image and text are read together.** A neutral sentence becomes a violation when combined
   with a suggestive image (e.g. "Ready for summer?" + a photo of a measuring tape around a waist).
3. **Implied claims count.** Meta penalises what an average user would *understand*, not only what
   is literally written.
4. **Personal attributes are the most common reason for rejection.** See section 5.
5. **The landing page must match the ad** and must not be broken, deceptive, or a different product.

---

## 2. Prohibited content (never allowed)

| Area | What is prohibited |
|---|---|
| Illegal products/services | Anything illegal in the targeted country. |
| Drugs & drug-related products | Illegal, prescription or recreational drugs, drug paraphernalia, "legal highs". |
| Tobacco & vaping | Cigarettes, e-cigarettes, vapes, rolling papers, hookah and related accessories. |
| Unsafe substances | Steroids, anabolic agents, chitosan, ephedra, human growth hormone, DMAA, and other unsafe supplements as determined by Meta. |
| Weapons, ammunition, explosives | Firearms, parts, ammunition, explosives, weapon modification accessories, non-lethal weapons for self-defence (pepper spray, stun guns) in most cases. |
| Adult products/services & adult content | Nudity, implied nudity, sexually suggestive poses, focus on body parts, sex toys (family-planning/contraception allowed with restrictions). |
| Discriminatory practices | Ads that discriminate or encourage discrimination based on personal attributes. |
| Deceptive, false or misleading content | Fake claims, fake offers, deceptive business practices, misleading pricing, "free" with hidden conditions, fake urgency. |
| Misinformation | Claims debunked by third-party fact-checkers, health misinformation. |
| Shocking, sensational, violent content | Gore, excessive violence, scary or disrespectful imagery, sensational shock tactics. |
| Sensational health "miracles" | Cures for serious diseases, "doctors hate this", unrealistic outcomes. |
| Low-quality / disruptive content | Clickbait, engagement bait ("tag 3 friends", "like if you agree"), withholding information to force a click, sensational headlines. |
| Spyware / malware / circumventing systems | Software that tracks users without consent; cloaking; techniques to bypass ad review. |
| Multi-level marketing / "get rich quick" | Income opportunities that promote compensation mainly from recruiting; promises of fast or effortless wealth. |
| Payday loans, bail bonds, cash advances | Short-term loans that are repaid in full on the borrower's next payday (≤ 90 days). |
| Penny auctions | Bid-fee auction models. |
| Counterfeit goods | Replicas or imitations of branded products. |
| Human body parts / fluids | Sale of organs, blood, etc. |
| Vaccine discouragement | Ads that discourage vaccination. |
| Grammar & profanity | Excessive profanity, deliberately broken grammar/symbols to evade review (e.g. "F.R.E.E", "l0se w3ight"). |
| Non-functional landing page | Pages that don't load, are under construction, or lead to a different product. |

## 3. Restricted content (allowed only under conditions)

General rule: restricted ads need **accurate targeting** (age/country), **compliance with local
law**, and often **prior written permission or authorization** from Meta.

### 3.1 Health, wellness and pharmaceuticals
- Prescription drugs, online pharmacies, telehealth: require Meta authorization (e.g. LegitScript)
  and are limited to certain countries; target 18+.
- OTC medicines: must comply with local laws; target 18+ in many regions.
- **No claims to cure, treat or prevent diseases** (diabetes, cancer, hypertension, COVID…) for
  supplements, herbs, cosmetics or "natural" products.
- No guaranteed or exaggerated health outcomes ("100% effective", "results in 3 days",
  "clinically proven" without proof).
- Cosmetic procedures and health products must not create a negative self-perception.

### 3.2 Weight loss, body image and fitness
- Weight-loss products/programs/supplements: **target 18+ only**.
- **Prohibited:** before-and-after images, images that zoom on body parts (belly, thighs, cellulite,
  "muffin top"), measuring tapes/scales used to imply results, images or text that idealise or shame
  body types.
- **Prohibited claims:** unrealistic or specific timeframes ("lose 10 kg in 2 weeks"), guaranteed
  results, "without diet or exercise", "melts fat", miracle language.
- Allowed: healthy-lifestyle messaging, showing people exercising or eating healthy food, neutral
  product photos, general and realistic statements ("supports a balanced diet").

### 3.3 Before-and-after images (all categories)
- Not allowed for weight loss, cosmetic procedures, health and body-related products.
- Includes side-by-side comparisons, split images, "day 1 / day 30", sliders, implied transformations
  and images that show an "ideal" result next to an "imperfect" one.
- Also risky for skin care (acne, wrinkles, pigmentation), hair loss, teeth whitening.

### 3.4 Cryptocurrency and financial products
- Crypto exchanges, wallets, trading platforms, lending, mining hardware: require **Meta written
  permission** and regulatory licences in the targeted countries. ICOs and binary options are
  prohibited.
- Financial and insurance products: must disclose fees/risks, comply with local law; no promises of
  guaranteed returns, "risk-free", "double your money", "passive income guaranteed".
- Forex, CFDs, investment opportunities with unrealistic returns → rejected.
- Personal attributes regarding finances ("Are you in debt?", "Bad credit?") are prohibited.

### 3.5 Loans, credit and financial services
- Payday loans and cash advances are prohibited (see section 2).
- Other loans/credit cards: must disclose APR, fees, repayment period, lender's physical address;
  many countries require "special ad category: credit" (limits targeting: no age, gender, zip-code
  targeting in some regions).
- No "guaranteed approval", "no credit check" style claims.

### 3.6 Dating
- Requires Meta written permission. Target 18+.
- Must not be sexually suggestive, focus on sexual content, or show suggestive imagery.
- No images of people in sexually suggestive poses, no "hook-up" or casual-sex positioning.

### 3.7 Alcohol
- Must comply with local laws and industry codes; target **18+ or 21+** depending on country
  (e.g. 21+ in the US); prohibited entirely in some countries (e.g. several MENA countries,
  including Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Libya, and others).
- Must not encourage excessive drinking, link drinking to success, attractiveness or driving,
  or show minors.

### 3.8 Gambling, gaming, lotteries
- Real-money gambling requires written permission and licences; targeting 18+.
- Social casino games: allowed with restrictions, no implication of real-money winnings.

### 3.9 Other restricted categories
- Online/real-money gaming, sweepstakes, contests (must include official rules).
- Subscription services (must disclose recurring billing and price clearly).
- Cosmetic procedures (18+, no before/after).
- Supplements (no unsafe ingredients, no disease claims, 18+ for weight-loss).
- Social issues, elections or politics: require authorization and "Paid for by" disclaimer.
- Employment, housing, credit (special ad categories): targeting restrictions.

## 4. Exaggerated, misleading or unrealistic claims

Flag any of the following, especially in health, beauty, finance, e-commerce:

- Absolute guarantees: "guaranteed", "100%", "always", "never fails", "risk-free".
- Unrealistic timeframes: "in 3 days", "overnight", "instantly".
- Superlatives without proof: "best in the world", "#1", "clinically proven", "doctor-recommended"
  (without verifiable evidence).
- Fake scarcity / urgency: countdowns that reset, "only 2 left" when untrue.
- Misleading prices: "free" when shipping or subscription is hidden; crossed-out "original" prices
  that never existed.
- Fake endorsements: celebrities or public figures who did not endorse the product; fake reviews;
  fake news-site layouts.
- Fake UI elements: fake play buttons, fake notifications, fake "close" buttons, fake system alerts.

**Safer alternatives:** "may help", "designed to", "supports", "many customers report", concrete
verifiable facts, transparent pricing.

## 5. Personal attributes (very common rejection reason)

Ads must **not assert or imply** that the viewer has a personal characteristic. Attributes include:
race/ethnic origin, religion or beliefs, age, sexual orientation/practices, gender identity,
disability, physical or mental health (including medical conditions, body weight), vulnerable
financial status, voting status, trade-union membership, criminal record, name.

**Prohibited examples:**
- "Are you fat?" / "Are you overweight?" / "Struggling with your belly fat?"
- "Do you suffer from diabetes?" / "Depressed?"
- "Meet other Muslim/Christian singles near you"
- "Over 50? You need this."
- "Are you in debt?" / "Bad credit?"
- Using "you/your" + a personal condition ("your acne", "your hair loss", "your anxiety").

**Allowed rewrites:**
- "Discover our healthy meal plans." / "Looking for a balanced lifestyle?"
- "Information about diabetes care." (no "you have")
- "Meet singles who share your interests." (no attribute)
- Speak about the product, not about the viewer's condition.

## 6. Intellectual property and third-party brands

- Do not use **another company's logo, trademark, brand name, or product images** in a way that
  suggests affiliation or endorsement, unless authorized.
- Do not use Meta brand assets (Facebook/Instagram logos, "Like" thumb) beyond Meta's brand
  guidelines; never imply Meta endorses the product.
- Counterfeits, "replica", "1:1 copy", "inspired by [brand]" with brand logos → prohibited.
- Copyrighted characters, celebrity photos, music, film stills without rights → risky.
- Comparisons with competitors must be truthful and not use their logos misleadingly.

---

## 7. Text on images

- Meta removed the strict "20% text rule" but still recommends minimal text: ads with **a lot of
  text on the image tend to get lower reach and worse delivery**, and heavy text can trigger closer
  review.
- Best practice: keep image text short (headline/price/brand), move detailed copy to the primary
  text field, avoid covering faces or the product.
- Text on image is still subject to **all other policies** (claims, personal attributes, etc.).

---

## 8. Images and creative quality

- No nudity, implied nudity, excessive skin, sexually suggestive poses even for clothing or
  cosmetics — focus on the product.
- No shocking, gory, scary, or disgusting images (e.g. close-up of fungus, wounds, parasites,
  medical procedures, cellulite).
- No images designed to create body-image anxiety.
- No fake buttons, fake video players, fake notifications.
- No excessive flashing/strobing (video).
- Children: must not be sexualized; ads targeting or depicting minors in restricted categories
  (alcohol, weight loss, dating) are prohibited.

---

## 9. Landing page & business integrity (for context)

- The landing page must work, be mobile-friendly, match the ad's product and price, and not
  auto-download files.
- No "cloaking" (showing reviewers a different page from users).
- Contact info, return policy and clear pricing reduce the risk of "low-quality / deceptive
  business" rejections and account restrictions.

---

## 10. Reviewer checklist (use for every ad)

1. What is the product? Is it prohibited, restricted, or normal?
2. Does the image show before/after, body-part zoom, nudity, shocking content, fake UI,
   third-party logos, too much text?
3. Does the text assert a personal attribute ("you", "your" + condition/identity)?
4. Are there exaggerated, guaranteed, time-bound or medical claims?
5. Is there engagement bait, clickbait, or deceptive urgency/pricing?
6. Does the ad need age targeting (18+/21+), special ad category, or Meta authorization?
7. Do image and text together imply something prohibited?
8. What concrete edits would make it compliant while keeping the marketing goal?

### Verdict guidelines
- **compliant**: no meaningful violation; at most best-practice tips (e.g. reduce image text).
- **medium_risk**: borderline wording, restricted category needing targeting/authorization,
  heavy text on image, ambiguous imagery, or claims that may be flagged depending on the reviewer.
- **non_compliant**: clear violation of a prohibited policy, personal attributes, before/after for
  body/health, guaranteed or medical claims, prohibited product, third-party trademark misuse.
